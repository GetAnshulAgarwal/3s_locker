Our Story

Why We Build Lockers

Shared spaces lose belongings, parcels pile up at reception desks and manual key systems fail under heavy footfall. We started 3S to solve that with lockers that are rugged, easy to use and sized for the way each site actually works.

  • Built from Mohkampur Industrial Estate, Meerut
  • Live installations in high-footfall locations such as Tripti Bhojnalya, Katra
  • Custom sizing instead of one-size-fits-all templates
