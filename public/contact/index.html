FAQ

Before you reach out.

How soon will I hear back?

We reply to every enquiry within one business day, usually sooner.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. Scoping your space and recommending a setup is free, with no obligation to buy.

Do you install outside Meerut?

Yes. Tell us your location and we will confirm installation and service arrangements.

What should I send you?

A short description is enough. Floor plans, photos and expected user numbers help us give a more accurate quote.

Can I see lockers before deciding?

You can visit our Meerut workshop by appointment, and we can share photos of live installations such as Tripti Bhojnalya, Katra.
