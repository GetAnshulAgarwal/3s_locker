FAQ

Retail, answered.

How do customers open the locker?

They scan a QR code, enter a PIN or use the 3S app, depending on how you configure the system.

Can lockers sit outside the store?

Yes. Outdoor lockers are weather-resistant and available around the clock.

Can we notify customers automatically?

Yes. Customers get a notification as soon as their order is placed in a locker.

What if an order is not collected?

Set a time limit and the system flags uncollected items for staff.

Can we add payment on pickup?

Yes, with UPI or card payments through our payment gateway integration.
