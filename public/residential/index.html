FAQ

Residential, answered.

Do residents need an app?

They can use the 3S app or a PIN or QR code sent by message.

Can multiple couriers use the system?

Yes. Couriers get drop-off access without seeing resident data.

Are lockers available outdoors?

Yes, outdoor lockers suit gate houses and building entrances.

How are residents notified?

Automatically, as soon as a parcel is dropped in a locker.

Can it integrate with our society software?

We offer integrations and an API to connect resident records.
