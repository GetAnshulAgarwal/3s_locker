Day Use Lockers

Shared, on-demand lockers for short stays. Users take any free compartment, keep it for a few hours and release it when they leave, so a small bank serves far more people than assigned lockers. Access works with an ID card, PIN, QR code or the 3S app, with automatic release and optional pay-per-use.

How It Works

Simple self-service, in three steps.

Every user follows the same easy flow, with nothing to install or learn.

1

Identify Yourself

Tap an ID card or badge, or use a PIN, QR code or the 3S app.

2

Store or Grab

A free locker opens automatically for storing, and one tap retrieves your items later.

3

Auto-Free

The locker returns to the shared pool after pickup, so many people can use the bank at once.

FAQ

Day Use Lockers, answered.

How is day-use different from assigned lockers?

Day-use lockers are not owned by anyone. Users take any free locker and it releases when they finish.

Do users need an app?

No. A badge, PIN or QR code works, and the app is an optional extra.

Can many people use the lockers at once?

Yes. The software handles simultaneous users and frees each locker automatically.

Can we charge for use?

Yes, with optional payment through UPI or card.
