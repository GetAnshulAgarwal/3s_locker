Package Lockers

Standard Lockers

Indoor smart locker banks that let tenants, employees, students and guests send, receive and store items on their own schedule. Each compartment has an electronic lock, opens with a PIN, QR code, ID card or the 3S app, and is tracked in the cloud, so parcels, documents and belongings stay safe without anyone at the desk.

  • ADA

How It Works

Simple self-service, in three steps.

Every user follows the same easy flow, with nothing to install or learn.

1

Drop Off

A courier or staff member scans the parcel and the system opens a compartment of the right size.

2

Get Notified

The recipient is alerted by app, SMS or email with a PIN or QR code.

3

Collect & Free Up

They collect in seconds, and the compartment returns to the pool for the next delivery.

FAQ

Standard Lockers, answered.

How do recipients open a locker?

With a PIN, QR code, ID card or the 3S app, depending on how you set the system up.

Can we choose compartment sizes?

Yes. We configure the mix of sizes around your typical items.

What if a parcel is not collected?

You set a time limit, and the system sends reminders and flags overdue items.

Do you install it?

Yes. Our team installs on site and trains your staff.
