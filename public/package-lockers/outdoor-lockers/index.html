How It Works

Simple self-service, in three steps.

Every user follows the same easy flow, with nothing to install or learn.

1

Arrive Any Time

Users reach the weather-sealed kiosk at any hour, with no staff on site.

2

Verify

A code, app or card confirms who they are and which compartment is theirs.

3

Open & Go

The compartment opens, they take their item and the door locks shut again.

FAQ

Are they weatherproof?

Yes. Powder-coated steel and sealed components are built for sun, dust and rain.

They need a basic power and network connection. Ask us about options for your site.
